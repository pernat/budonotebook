# Training Session — 2024-11-15

## Techniques practiced

- [[Ikkyo]] (Katate-dori, both omote and ura) — drilled approximately 40 minutes
- [[Irimi-nage]] (Shomen-uchi) — second half of class
- Ukemi practice — high falls warm-up

## Key observations

- In Ikkyo omote, sensei corrected my leading hand — I was pulling instead of guiding. The wrist should *invite*, not drag.
- Irimi-nage clicked for the first time when I stopped thinking about the throw and focused on the entering step (irimi). When the entry is right, the throw happens naturally.
- Training with Maria (shorter partner) made me notice I was bending down — need to maintain my own center regardless of uke's height.

## Things to work on next time

- Keep the leading wrist soft in Ikkyo — no tension
- Practice the entering step for Irimi-nage solo at home (just the footwork, no partner needed)
- Ask sensei about the difference between Ikkyo and Ikkyo-osae (the pin)
