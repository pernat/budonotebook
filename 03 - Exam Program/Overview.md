## How to use this section

- Open your current rank page

## Key terminology

| Japanese | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Omote | Front/forward entry |
| Ura | Rear/circular entry |
| Suwari-waza | Seated technique (both uke and nage seated) |
| Hanmi-handachi | Half-seated (nage seated, uke standing) |
| Jiyu-waza | Free technique |
| Randori | Multiple attacker free practice |
| Uke | The person who attacks and receives the technique |
| Nage (or Tori) | The person who applies the technique |
