# Nidan — 2. dan

Kandidati moraju razumjeti tehničku terminologiju Aikido-a, te znati biografske podatke o osnivaču. Kandidati moraju imati položen Shodan Buki Waza.

Candidates must understand the technical terminology of Aikido and know biographical data about the founder. Candidates must have passed Shodan Buki Waza.

## Ki-no-nagare serija / Ki-no-nagare series

Sve sljedeće tehnike izvode se u ki-no-nagare obliku (tečni, kontinuirani oblik bez zaustavljanja).

All of the following techniques are performed in ki-no-nagare form (flowing, continuous form without pausing).
