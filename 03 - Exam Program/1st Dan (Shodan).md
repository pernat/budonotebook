# 1st Dan — Shodan

> The first black belt grade. This is not an end point — it marks the beginning of serious study. The exam tests the full breadth of kyu content plus randori (multiple attackers).

---

## Exam format

Shodan exams vary by federation and country. The following is a common structure — confirm with your instructor.

1. **Suwari-waza** — demonstration of seated techniques
2. **Hanmi-handachi** — half-seated techniques
3. **Tachi-waza** — full standing technique set
4. **Tachi-dori / Jo-dori** — weapon taking
5. **Jiyu-waza** — free technique (single attacker)
6. **Randori** — multiple attacker practice *(sometimes required at Shodan, sometimes from Nidan)*

---

## Technical requirements

All kyu-level techniques must be performable on request. Key areas examiners focus on:

### Posture and basics

- [ ] Stable kamae in all positions
- [ ] Correct ma-ai (distance) management
- [ ] Relaxed, connected movement — no muscle tension
- [ ] Clean irimi and tenkan footwork

### Suwari-waza set

- [ ] Shomen-uchi Ikkyo — omote & ura
- [ ] Shomen-uchi Nikyo — omote & ura
- [ ] Shomen-uchi Sankyo — omote & ura
- [ ] Shomen-uchi Yonkyo — omote & ura
- [ ] Yokomen-uchi Ikkyo — omote & ura

### Hanmi-handachi set

- [ ] Shomen-uchi Ikkyo
- [ ] Katate-dori Shiho-nage — omote & ura
- [ ] Katate-dori Nikyo — omote & ura
- [ ] Katate-dori Irimi-nage
- [ ] Katate-dori Kaiten-nage
- [ ] Morote-dori Irimi-nage

### Tachi-waza — all previous plus

- [ ] Kata-dori Menuchi (full set)
- [ ] Ryote-dori (full set)
- [ ] Ushiro attacks (full set)

### Weapon taking

- [ ] Tachi-dori (sword disarming — multiple techniques)
- [ ] Jo-dori (staff disarming — multiple techniques)

### Jiyu-waza / Randori

- [ ] Confident jiyu-waza — fluid, controlled, no hesitation
- [ ] Randori awareness — maintaining center, reading multiple uke

---

## Ukemi requirements

- [ ] Confident high falls from all major techniques
- [ ] Able to receive Kote-gaeshi and Irimi-nage at speed
- [ ] Able to take Sankyo and Yonkyo pins

## Notes on the process

- Shodan is awarded, not taken — your instructor nominates you
- Grading before a panel is normal; some federations require a minimum membership period
- Stay patient and keep training; readiness is judged by your instructor, not self-assessed
