# About the Style

This vault follows the **Iwama Takemusu Aikido** tradition — the legacy of **Morihiro Saito Sensei**, who was the closest disciple of O-Sensei Morihei Ueshiba.

## Iwama Style Characteristics

Iwama style (also known as Iwama Ryu or Takemusu Aikido) is characterized by:

1.  **Kihon (Basics)**: A strong emphasis on solid, precise basic forms before moving to flowing movement.
2.  **Buki Waza (Weapons)**: The integration of Aiki-ken (wooden sword) and Aiki-jo (wooden staff) as fundamental to understanding empty-hand techniques.
3.  **Riai**: The principle that the movements of the sword, staff, and empty hands are identical.

## Lineage

-   **Morihei Ueshiba (O-Sensei)**: Founder of Aikido.
-   **Morihiro Saito Sensei**: 9th dan, spent 23 years living and training with the founder in Iwama.
-   **Hitohira Saito Sensei**: Son and successor, continuing the tradition through the Iwama Shin Shin Aiki Shuren Kai.

Many organizations worldwide continue to practice the Saito Sensei lineage within the Aikikai or as independent federations.
