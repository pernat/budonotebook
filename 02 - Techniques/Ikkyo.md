# Ikkyo

## Description
Ikkyo is the first basic arm pin, controlling uke through the elbow and wrist.

## Key Points
- Lead with your center.
- Control both the elbow and the wrist.
- The pin should be at shoulder level (90° angle).

## Variations
- **Omote**: Front entry, irimi step.
- **Ura**: Rear entry, tenkan pivot.
