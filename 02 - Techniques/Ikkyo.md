# Ikkyo

**Japanese:** 一教 (いっきょう)
**English:** First teaching / First control
**Attack:** Shomen-uchi, Katate-dori, Yokomen-uchi, Morote-dori
**Category:** Pin (osae-waza)
**First required at:** [[6th Kyu (Rokkyu)]]

---

## Description

Ikkyo is the first of the five basic arm pins (Ikkyo through Gokyo). It controls uke through the elbow and wrist, taking their balance and guiding them to the ground into a pin. It appears across all attack types and in suwari-waza and hanmi-handachi forms.

---

## Key points

- Lead with your center, not your hands — the arms follow body movement
- Control the elbow, not just the wrist — two control points
- The pin is at the shoulder level: uke's arm at 90°, hand palm-down
- Maintain a stable, low stance throughout

## Common mistakes

- Pulling or muscling the arm — Ikkyo should work with uke's momentum
- Standing too upright during the pin — lower your hips
- Releasing the elbow control during the transition to the ground
- Forgetting tenkan (pivot) in ura, turning with the arm instead of the whole body

---

## Omote (front entry)

Enter to the outside of uke's arm with irimi step. Control the elbow with one hand, guide the wrist with the other. Lead uke down and forward into the pin. Uke's arm forms a 90° angle at the shoulder.

## Ura (rear entry)

Blend with the attack using tenkan (pivot). Use uke's momentum to guide them in a circular path. The pin position is the same as omote.
