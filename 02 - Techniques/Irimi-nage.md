# Irimi-nage

## Description
Irimi-nage is a blending entry that places nage behind or alongside uke, followed by a throw using the body's momentum.

## Key Points
- The entering step (irimi) must be decisive.
- Align your shoulder with uke's shoulder.
- Throw with your hips, not your arms.

## Variations
- **Omote**: Front entry, blending with uke's power.
- **Ura**: Rear entry, using tenkan to lead uke around.
